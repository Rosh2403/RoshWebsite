MuseBoard: porting Muse to a round ESP32-S3 display

A step-by-step walkthrough of porting Meta's Muse gadget firmware to a Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.46B: power latch, IO expander, a round QSPI display, touch, a battery overflow, a speaker that wouldn't speak, and spoken replies. The board support is now merged into Meta's repo.
